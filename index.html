Why the 2026 elections matter

Every election is a chance to influence the decisions that affect everyday life.

Leaders set priorities

Elected officials decide how public money is spent, what policies are adopted, and which communities receive support and attention.

Voting protects democracy

Participation keeps leaders accountable and ensures the government reflects the people it serves instead of only a small group of interests.

Your vote has impact

Elections can be decided by very small margins. In many communities, a single vote can influence school funding, public safety, transportation, and housing policies.

Issues on the ballot

These are just a few of the areas voters can influence in 2026.

Economy and jobs

  • Wages, inflation, and cost of living
  • Small business support and local growth
  • Workforce training and job opportunities

Education

  • School funding and teacher support
  • College affordability and student resources
  • Access to safe, high-quality learning spaces

Healthcare

  • Affordable care and coverage access
  • Public health and emergency preparedness
  • Support for families and vulnerable communities

Community safety and justice

  • Public safety investments
  • Equitable law enforcement and accountability
  • Programs that support safe neighborhoods

Make a plan to vote

Voting is not only a right, it is a responsibility that strengthens our democracy.

1. Check your registration

Make sure your voter registration is current before the election deadline.

2. Learn the ballot

Research candidates and measures so your vote reflects your values and priorities.

3. Vote on time

Whether in person, early, or by mail, choose the method that works best for you and complete it before the deadline.

Your vote shapes the future. Be informed, be prepared, and make your voice heard in 2026.
